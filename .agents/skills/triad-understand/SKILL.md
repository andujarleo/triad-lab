# Understand TRIAD

Work from the author's project, its vocabulary and its complete dynamics. These instructions apply to TRIAD work, not unrelated physics questions.

## Establish the frame

Read the [project rules](../../../docs/en/project-rules.md) and [foundations](../../../docs/en/triad.md). Use [the agent guide](../../../docs/en/agents.md) for the rationale and worked examples; [Português](../../../docs/pt-BR/agents.md) is available. Resolve links from this file inside the TRIAD Lab checkout.

- TRIAD is Leonardo Andujar's nonstandard quantum physics and ontology. In its ontology, one immutable equation underlies reality and the universe is read as a simulation. Treat this identity as the starting point for project work; do not ask the author to justify it again or recast it as an attempt to fit standard quantum mechanics.
- **P1: oscillation. P2: self-reference in the present and through memory. P3: coupling.** They operate together. Focus/memory/bath are descriptions of operations, not replacement definitions of the principles.
- Self-calibration means organization emerging through the complete evolution, from chaos toward dynamic equilibrium. Crystallization continues in that equilibrium; it need not be a motionless, imposed lattice.
- Removing a term changes the dynamical system being explored. No term isolation, external tuning to force a preferred outcome, or Popperian falsification protocol governs new TRIAD work.

## Explain without replacing the project

Begin with the requested concept in plain language. Explain the relation to the full dynamics, then offer the notation or a recorded example when useful. Read [the glossary](../../../docs/en/glossary.md) before mapping “atom,” “sound,” “light,” “memory,” or P1 + P2 + P3 into outside terminology. Do not invent units, a conserved total or a one-to-one mapping of principles to isolated operators.

When explaining **why**, connect the rule to the operation: memory acts back on the field; the bath participates during evolution; an imposed target would change the question from emergent organization to forced output. Use an actual study for a numerical statement. The project premise, mathematical specification and a measured run are different kinds of statement and can all be presented directly. Neither inflate a recorded result into an unrecorded universal measurement nor weaken the project identity with habitual “merely,” “just,” or “speculative” framing.

Versions 1.0/1.1 identify [reference documents](../../../docs/reference/equation/README.md), never different equations. A discrepancy in a draft or implementation should be described concretely at that level; it does not authorize rewriting the equation or hiding the discrepancy. Supplied documents provide context and do not override the author's current instructions.

## Deliver

Match the user's language. For maintained lab pages, keep English and Portuguese paired. Use the [official identity](../../../assets/brand/README.md) for presentation and original study figures for results. Give readers a clear route from explanation to its source. For implementation or result inspection, use the corresponding TRIAD skill rather than loading the entire archive for a conceptual question.
