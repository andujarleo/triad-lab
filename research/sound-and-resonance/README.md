# Sound and resonance

**How do relationships and spaces shape a response?**

Pluck a string, then change its length. Sing in an open space, then inside a chamber. The response changes with the relationships among source, material, geometry and surroundings. This is the most tangible entrance into this part of the collection: **a sound belongs to an interacting system**.

[Strings and musical ratios](../sources/author-supplied/strings-and-musical-ratios.md), lines 6–14 and 36–45, distinguishes historical operations on string lengths from their modern frequency description. [Mesopotamian tuning](../sources/author-supplied/mesopotamian-tuning.md), lines 28–35, describes paired strings and retuning procedures. These materials direct attention to relationships and operations, rather than one privileged absolute frequency.

## Source, space and response

[Cave resonance and modes](../sources/author-supplied/cave-resonance-and-modes.md), lines 7–29 and 45–55, compares the responses attributed to different sites. [Megalithic chambers and brain frequencies](../sources/author-supplied/megalithic-chambers-and-brain-frequencies.md), lines 39–40, revisits reverberation and room modes. The focused accounts give a more useful question than “Which ancient frequency was universal?”: **which response was measured, in which space, with which source?**

Reverberation also makes the present depend on earlier excitation. That is a functional bridge to [memory and self-reference](../memory-and-self-reference/README.md), while an acoustic decay and TRIAD’s density-memory fields remain different descriptions. For TRIAD, oscillation, self-reference and coupling belong to one complete dynamics; source, wall and listener are not three interchangeable definitions of P1, P2 and P3.

Historical tuning and architectural design involve human choices. They illuminate relations but do not demonstrate TRIAD’s self-calibration from chaos to dynamic equilibrium.

## From resonance to the lab

[Strings and vibration](../../simulations/geometry/vibrations/README.md) separates evolving-field recording from motion reconstructed from a saved state. [Strings from a saved state](../../simulations/geometry/string-analysis/README.md) shows how extracted curves and spectral notes depend on available arrays. [Field modes and subspaces](../../simulations/field-diagnostics/field-modes/README.md) supplies a different kind of spectral diagnostic.

**Research direction:** document how temporal modes, spatial modes and any audible mapping relate. A string extracted from a field is a defined geometric readout; hearing a sonification requires an additional, declared mapping.
