# Signals and interpretation

**What does a representation reveal about a process?**

A convincing image is the end of a chain. Something was recorded, processed, selected and represented before we saw it. Understanding that chain makes a figure more useful: it tells us **which part of a process is visible and how it became visible**.

The [Giza SAR report](../sources/author-supplied/giza-sar-signal-and-reconstruction.md), lines 34–38, makes this distinction between instrument, processing and inference. It belongs here as a methodological reading about reconstruction; the supplied document includes no original radar dataset or TRIAD execution. [Sound, symbol and transmission](../sources/author-supplied/sound-symbol-and-transmission.md), lines 446–448 and 657–687, asks a related question of artifacts and replicas: what survives, what is reconstructed and what can be inferred?

## Follow the representation

| Step | What a reader needs |
|---|---|
| Recorded source | The state, time window, arrays, units and conditions actually available. |
| Transformation | The extraction, projection, normalization or audio mapping applied. |
| Display | What a color, curve, slice, spectrum or sound represents. |
| Interpretation | The claim made from that readout and the observations it uses. |

This is also how the lab makes its own material inspectable. [Strings from a saved state](../../simulations/geometry/string-analysis/README.md) documents which arrays are present and why phase strings are skipped for the included state. [The first phase-to-density diagnostic](../../simulations/signals/first-phase-to-density-diagnostic/README.md) and [the refined diagnostic](../../simulations/signals/refined-phase-to-density-diagnostic/README.md) preserve a failed estimator and its later historical comparison together.

The [rerun dossier](../../simulations/numerical-checks/reproduction-dossier/README.md) distinguishes measuring a lattice detector’s response from retuning equation coefficients. Its historical term-off protocols remain historical records; they do not become the method for new TRIAD work.

P1, P2 and P3 remain together in the equation while a display selects an aspect of a record. Technical inspection asks whether that selection and its provenance are stated accurately. It does not replace TRIAD’s identity or method with an external criterion.

**Research direction:** make each future research figure a route back to its source and transformation. Keep measured dynamics, reconstructed motion, conceptual illustrations and planned experiments identifiable at a glance.
