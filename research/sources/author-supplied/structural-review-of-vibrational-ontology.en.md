## Details

### 1) EQUATION PHYSICS

**1.1 Equation structure.** The proposed equation — iħ∂ₜΨ = [-∇² + (-Δ)^(σ/2) + Λ|Ψ|²Ψ + V_ext − iΓ + η + V_mem]Ψ with ∂ₜy_j = ν_j(|Ψ|² − y_j) — is, term by term, a legitimate and recognizable combination of standard models. The core iħ∂ₜΨ = -∇²Ψ + Λ|Ψ|²Ψ is the cubic Gross-Pitaevskii/NLS equation, a universal model for weakly nonlinear dispersive systems (Bose-Einstein condensates, Kerr optics). **CONFIRMED** as structure.

**1.2 Cubic nonlinearity and U(1) gauge.** The claim that |Ψ|²Ψ is the lowest-order nonlinearity compatible with global U(1) gauge invariance (Ψ→e^{iθ}Ψ) is **CORRECT**. A nonlinearity preserving phase symmetry must depend only on |Ψ|²; the lowest non-trivial order term in the equation (derived from energy ∝|Ψ|⁴) is |Ψ|²Ψ. Terms like Ψ² or |Ψ|Ψ would break U(1) or be non-analytic.

**1.3 Fractional Laplacian.** (-Δ)^(σ/2) is the Riesz operator of the fractional Schrödinger equation, introduced by Nick Laskin (Phys. Rev. E 62, 3135, 2000; Phys. Rev. E 66, 056108, 2002) by extending Feynman's path integral from Brownian trajectories to Lévy flights. In Fourier, it acts as |k|^σ. **CONFIRMED.** Important note: in the literature, fractional NLS can STABILIZE solitons that would be unstable in the σ=2 case (Stephanovich et al., *Scientific Reports* s41598-021-04292-7: "even infinitesimal difference α from 2 immediately stabilizes the soliton texture") — relevant to the document's anti-collapse thesis.

**1.4 Auxiliary-field memory and the SSM isomorphism.** This is the document's most solid and original contribution. The equation ∂ₜy_j = ν_j(|Ψ|² − y_j) is literally a first-order exponential low-pass filter: y_j(t) = ν_j ∫ e^{-ν_j(t-s)} |Ψ(s)|² ds. This is EXACTLY a single-pole diagonal State Space Model (SSM), of the form h'(t)=Ah(t)+Bu(t) with diagonal A (Gu et al., HiPPO/S4, 2020-2021). The bridge to S4/Mamba/HiPPO is **CONFIRMED** and not forced: diagonal models (DSS, S4D) showed a diagonal A matrix reproduces the essence of S4, and Mamba uses S4D-Real initialization. The document is correct to identify its memory potential with a diagonal SSM; what does not yet exist in the literature is that bridge applied specifically to a Ψ field with the physical sense of repulsive memory. That is **genuine original research**.

**1.5 Anti-collapse by delayed memory.** The literature confirms the GENERAL mechanism: (a) Bang, Krolikowski, Wyller & Rasmussen (Phys. Rev. E 66, 046619, 2002) prove RIGOROUSLY, by bounding the Hamiltonian, that nonlinearity non-locality prevents collapse in BEC and Kerr media in all physical dimensions, for symmetric non-local response of arbitrary form (provided the non-local potential's Fourier spectrum is absolutely integrable); (b) nonlinearity saturation, multiphoton absorption, group-velocity dispersion, plasma defocusing, and delayed Raman response are all proven collapse-arrest mechanisms (Couairon & Mysyrowicz review; Kosareva et al., Opt. Lett. 36, 1035, 2011). Raman response is precisely a delayed material response. HOWEVER: the document's SPECIFIC claim — that a first-order exponential filter over |Ψ|² generating a delayed repulsive potential prevents blow-up — does not appear in that exact form in the literature. The mechanism is plausible and aligned with known results (memory acts as a defocusing temporal non-locality), but it requires of the author its own virial proof or at least numerical evidence with explicit conditions on Λ<0 (or the repulsive-coupling sign), ν_j, and coupling strength. **PARTIAL.** Blow-up criteria: Glassey/virial, critical mass in 2D (Townes soliton, critical mass ‖Q‖²), supercritical in 3D — all standard and correctly invocable.

**1.6 Fluctuation-dissipation.** The relation between Γ (dissipation) and η amplitude (noise) is the heart of the stochastic Gross-Pitaevskii equation (SGPE). SGPE obeys an equilibrium version of the fluctuation-dissipation theorem, guaranteeing the condensate relaxes to the correct physical equilibrium (bath temperature T and chemical potential μ) — see Stoof and collaborators (cond-mat/0107432), and the derivation in which Keldysh self-energy simultaneously determines dissipation and fluctuation strength. The document's claim that this prevents both vacuum decay and unbounded heating is **CONFIRMED in rigor**: that is exactly the theorem's role — without noise the system would decay; without dissipation it would heat; their fixed ratio fixes the temperature.

**1.7 Bogoliubov and dispersion.** A condensate's Bogoliubov spectrum has the form E(k)=√[(ħ²k²/2m)(ħ²k²/2m + 2gn)]. In the low-k limit (low energies), E≈ħc_s k (linear/"relativistic"/acoustic — phonons); at high k, E≈ħ²k²/2m (parabolic/free particle). The document's claim that the relativistic/hyperbolic-dispersion analogy holds only in the low-energy limit is **CORRECT**. The contrast with free-Schrödinger parabolic dispersion and with the photon-baryon fluid (linear, c_s≈c/√3) is physically adequate.

Lett.* 122, 130405), and Pfau (Stuttgart, *Phys. Rev. The analogy to an exponential-decay memory potential is **PARTIALLY defensible**: there is indeed accumulation (perfuming) and decay/maturation dynamics (seeds are not static — "it is a flow, not a store"). ### 4) ARCHAEOACOUSTICS — the most fragile section

**4.1 What the document claims.** That megalithic sites (Ħal-Saflieni, Newgrange, King's Chamber, Göbekli Tepe) resonate at ~110 Hz; that 110 Hz deactivates the left temporal lobe/Wernicke's area and shifts dominance to the right prefrontal; and — skeptically and more defensibly — that 110 Hz is an EXAPTATION (mechanical consequence of dimensions, ritualized afterwards).

**4.2 The measurements.** The 95-120 Hz resonances are real as MEASUREMENTS, but of heterogeneous quality. Peer-reviewed primary source: Jahn, Devereux & Ibison, "Acoustical Resonances of Assorted Ancient Structures," *J. Acoust. Soc. Am.* 99(2):649-658 (Feb. 1996), DOI 10.1121/1.414642, verbatim: "measurements performed inside six diverse ancient structures revealed that each sustained a strong resonance at a frequency between 95 and 120 Hz, despite major differences in chamber shapes and sizes... strong antinodes at the outer walls" (preceded by PEAR Technical Report 95002, 1995). They measured six Neolithic/Iron Age structures (Wayland's Smithy, Chun Quoit, Cairn Euny in the UK; Newgrange and Cairns L and I in Ireland). Ħal-Saflieni "Oracle Room" ~110 Hz is attributed to measurements by composer Ruben Zahra with an Italian team and to SB Research Group (Debertolis & Eneix, 2014) — mostly NOT peer-reviewed. King's Chamber: Tom Danley (acoustic engineer) measured eigenmodes ~121 Hz; John Stuart Reid measured ~121 Hz in the chamber and ~117 Hz in the granite "sarcophagus," with beating between them — but these measurements circulate mostly in popular/alternative literature, not reviewed journals. Göbekli Tepe (160/81 Hz): attributed to Debertolis (2017), not robustly peer-reviewed. **Verdict: the ~95-120 Hz measurements have a real basis (Jahn et al. 1996, peer-reviewed); per-site specific values and intentionality are CONTESTED or UNVERIFIABLE.** Critical warning: today's measured resonances reflect the chambers' CURRENT state (without original caps, with rubble removed/added, different humidity), not necessarily the original state.

**4.3 The Cook et al. (2008) study.** Cook IA, Pajot SK, Leuchter AF, "Ancient Architectural Acoustic Resonance Patterns and Regional Brain Activity," *Time and Mind* 1(1):95-104 (Mar. 2008), DOI 10.2752/175169608783489099. Verbatim abstract text: "In a pilot project, 30 healthy adults listened to tones at 90, 100, 110, 120, and 130 Hz while brain activity was monitored with electroencephalography (EEG). Activity in the left temporal region was found to be significantly lower at 110 Hz than at other frequencies." The metric used was "cordance" (proprietary qEEG, co-invented by Cook and Leuchter). Statistics: lower left-temporal at 110 Hz (p=0.04 vs. 90 Hz; p=0.01 vs. 130 Hz; trend p=0.07 at 100 Hz — paired p-values NOT corrected for multiple comparisons), and prefrontal asymmetry shift from left dominance to right at 110 Hz. **Severe critical assessment:** (a) n=30, self-labeled pilot; (b) published in an interdisciplinary humanities/archaeology journal, NOT neuroscience; (c) marginal p-values without correction; (d) authors' own proprietary metric; (e) scalp EEG is weak regional inference vs. fMRI; (f) NOT pre-registered; (g) NEVER independently and adequately powerfully replicated. **4.4 The exaptation hypothesis and the physics.** Here the document commits a physical error needing correction. For a rigid-wall chamber, the fundamental axial mode is f = c/(2L), with c ≈ 343 m/s (at 20 °C, ISO 9613-1) and Neumann boundary condition (zero normal velocity, v_n|_Σ=0 ⟹ ∂Φ/∂n|_Σ=0 at the rigid wall — producing pressure antinodes at walls, exactly the pattern reported by Jahn et al. and confirmed by Snakowska, *Archives of Acoustics* 32(4), 2007). Explicit calculation:
- L = 2 m → f = 343/4 = **85.75 Hz**
- L = 3 m → f = 343/6 = **57.2 Hz**
- L = 4 m → f = 343/8 = **42.9 Hz**

To get 110 Hz as axial fundamental would require L = 343/220 = **1.56 m**. Therefore, for 2–4 m spans, the fundamental mode falls at **~43-86 Hz, well below 110 Hz**. The document's math does NOT close: 110 Hz in a 2-4 m span would only appear as a HIGHER-ORDER mode, oblique/tangential mode (3D formula: f_lmn = (c/2)·√[(l/Lx)²+(m/Ly)²+(n/Lz)²]), or as the fundamental of only the smallest dimension (~1.5 m). **The precise claim needs correction:** either the relevant span is ~1.5 m (plausible for narrow niches/passages, like the Oracle Room's oval "niche"), or 110 Hz is a higher mode, or Helmholtz resonance intervenes (depending on volume and "neck," not c/2L). The popular narrative "110 Hz = natural resonance of 2-4 m megalithic chambers" is physically fragile. What IS defensible in the exaptation thesis is the general principle: resonances are a mechanical consequence of dimensions and materials, and any ritual meaning was attributed a posteriori — this correctly inverts the mystical narrative. **PARTIAL: good exaptation thesis, wrong specific arithmetic.**

**4.5 RT60, Neumann, and infrasound.** Reverberation times (RT60) measured at megalithic sites exist in the literature (e.g., Marshall, "Acoustics of the West Kennet Long Barrow," *Time and Mind* 9(1):43-56, 2016). ### 6) PHILOSOPHY OF SCIENCE

**6.1 Ontic Structural Realism (OSR).** Ladyman, Ross, and French: the position holds that what fundamentally exists are relations/structures, not objects with intrinsic properties ("structure is ontologically basic"; "there are no individuals, there is relational structure" — Ladyman 1998; French & Ladyman 2003; Ladyman & Ross, *Every Thing Must Go*, 2007). The document's thesis that mathematics, megalithic architecture, and brain share "the same structural universality class" is where there is **conceptual confusion to correct**. One must distinguish:
- **Universality in the technical sense** (renormalization group, critical exponents, universality classes in phase transitions): physically distinct systems exhibit the SAME critical exponents by sharing dimensionality and symmetries. This is rigorous and verifiable.
- **Metaphorical/analogical use** ("everything is the same vibratory structure"): formal isomorphism (the same equations describing different systems) is NOT ontological identity (the systems being the same thing).

The document repeatedly slides from formal isomorphism to physical/ontological identity. OSR, correctly read, authorizes saying structure is what is real and transferable across theories — but NOT that a stone chamber and the cortex are "the same" because both have resonances, nor that NLS and Spanda describe the same entity. **The needed correction:** keep the formal isomorphisms (legitimate and fertile) and explicitly mark each point where one jumps to ontological identity (unjustified). A 110 Hz resonance in a stone and a 110 Hz EEG alteration do not belong to the same "universality class" in the technical sense — they are just two systems responding to the same input frequency.
