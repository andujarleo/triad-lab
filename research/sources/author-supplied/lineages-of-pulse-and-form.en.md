Sulba Sutras (c. 800–300 BC, much older practice).

Sulba = cord. Fire-altar geometry: falcon, tortoise, rhombus, circle, equal-area square — as school theorem. Staal argued a common ritual origin of Indian and Greek geometry: form does not illustrate the rite; form is the rite. Sound (Vedic mantra) + geometry (brick veda) are the same sacrifice. Concrete coupling: cord, peg, sacrificer's body as unit of measure.

Yantra + mantra.

"Yantra is mantra seen; mantra is yantra heard." The Sri Yantra: nine interlaced triangles around the bindu. The bindu is the dimensionless point — your monad, the compass's fixed foot. The triangles are Shiva/Shakti: coupled consciousness and energy. Each diagram region corresponds to a syllable. Form and frequency do not add up; they are one. That is later in classical form, but the sound/figure pair is the Veda's direct unfolding.

Spanda (Kashmir, 9th century, barest doctrine).

Spanda = throb, consciousness's creative pulse. Not metaphor. Consciousness does not illuminate a ready world: the world is consciousness pulsing upon itself (vimarsa = self-reflexivity). Light (prakasa) and pulse are the two faces of the same. Here self-reference stops being a modern term: the absolute knows itself by vibrating itself. Oscillation + loop + manifestation. The closest, in philosophical language, to what your equation writes with A/2 and iℏ∂t.

2. Egypt — word, heart, mound, cord

Memphite creation (Ptah): the world is thought in the heart (Sia, intellection) and spoken by the tongue (Hu, efficacious utterance). Not "God speaks and matter obeys". Naming is giving form. Thoth is lord of the divine word and of time's measure. The first object emerging from the waters is the benben — pyramidal mound, geometry of the first solid. Ptah is the cosmic Architect: what coagulates chaos into figure according to Maat (equilibrium).

Architecture follows a proportion canon (1:2, 1:4 triangles, sequences of significant points on the temple axis). The Egyptian "sacred cord" is the same instrument as the Sulba: field geometry. The eye (Ra, Horus) is the Sun as organ of seeing — your light/eye chain is already in the pantheon, not only in the lodge.

The Egyptian sequence: breath/name → form → solar light. Sound precedes the visible.

3. Mesopotamia — gods as musical ratios

Paleo-Babylonian texts (~1800 BC) name string pairs (dichords), seven heptatonic modes, tighten/loosen procedures. A Neo-Babylonian text draws a seven-pointed star with the strings' names — geometry of the tuning cycle.

Ernest McClain and others read the Sumerian pantheon in base 60 as an acoustic model: gods' numbers = ratios. Even whoever rejects the maximal reading admits the minimum fact: temple, calendar, and tuning use the same ratio arithmetic. Sky-cord-brick coupling. The Greek tetraktys, in this reading, is a late cut from an older matrix (pebble/brick triangle of the mountain ideogram).

Here sound is not the myth's "soundtrack". It is the language in which the myth calculates.

4. China — the pipe measuring the world

The huangzhong (Yellow Bell) is not just a note. It is the unit of length, volume, and weight. Millet grains fill the pipe; the pipe defines the chi; from the huangzhong the twelve lü are born by the "add or remove a third" rule (fifth/fourth, the same 3:2 and 4:3). The twelve pipes map months, directions, qi. Jing Fang (1st century BC) buries pipes with ash: when the season's qi matches the tone, the ash flies. "Heaven models itself by shadow; Earth, by echo."

Geometry + sound + calendar + breath (qi) as one system. Pipe oscillation = year oscillation. Institutional coupling: the empire tunes itself on the same object with which cloth is measured.

5. Plato / Timaeus — the world soul is a scale

The Demiurge does not paint the sky. He cuts the world soul in the ratios 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 9, 27 (two progressions: octaves and twelfths), fills with harmonic and arithmetic means, and obtains 3:2, 4:3, 9:8. Then he folds the strip into two circles (same / other = equator / ecliptic). The Platonic solids clothe the four elements. The initial chaos is said without music. To order is to tune.

That is Pythagoras turned into explicit cosmogony: solids' geometry + circles' geometry + cord's geometry = a single Demiurge. Your scheme's architect.

6. Sefer Yetzirah — letter, number, breath

Late Hebrew text (probably between the 2nd and 6th century), but the principle is the same as Genesis read from inside: "Let there be" is a vibrated letter.

God engraves the Name through three books: text (sefer), number (sefar), account/sound (sippur). 22 letters + 10 sefirot = 32 paths. The letters are "carved in air", combined into 231 gates on a circle oscillating back and forth. Aleph, Mem, Shin = air, water, fire — sound mothers generating world, year, and body. Geometry here is the letters' circle and the figures the voice draws in air (medieval commentaries speak of triangles and squares according to the mouth's articulation point).

Sound → combination → form. Self-reference: the Name folds over the letters it is made of.
