The chain you assembled is not a secret code. It is the same object seen through two axes that the lodge already places in the same place: the East. One axis is luminous. The other is constructive. They meet in the Delta.

Axis 1 — G → Sun → Light → Eye

G → Sun

Established: G = Geometry in the document; G = the note Sol on the staff; Sol = the heavenly body in Latin. In the lodge the G often stands over the blazing sun. This is not etymology. It is composition.

Sun → Light

The Sun is the visible source. Masonic initiation does not speak of "learning a dogma"; it speaks of being led from darkness into Light. The three lesser lights, the blazing star, the "glory" at the center, the Venerable in the East (the rising place): all of this is the same gesture. In 1813 the approved English ritual says bluntly that the blazing star "refers us to the sun, which enlightens the earth". Sun and Light, here, are not parallel metaphors. One is the body, the other is the act.

Light → Eye

The eye does not produce light. The eye receives light. Without light there is no vision; without vision light does not become knowledge. That is why traditions treat the Sun as the eye of heaven: Ovid ("the sun is the eye of nature"), Milton ("Thou sun... both eye and soul"), Vedic hymns to Surya as the "All-Seeing Eye". Masonry distinguishes the two emblems — Sun is one thing, the All-Seeing Eye is another — but places them in the same field: the Eye is "he whom the Sun, the Moon and the Stars obey". The eye is Light turned into vigilance.

Open eye = work done.

Historically the eye in the triangle arrives late (18th century, Christian recycling of the Eye of Providence). The Sun and the G are older in the Craft. The luminous chain is therefore a later condensation of pieces already in the room, not the founding meaning of the letter G.

Axis 2 — Compass → Triangle → Base → Architect

Compass

The instrument that traces the circle from a point. One foot fixed (center), the other turns (periphery). Proverbs 8:27: "when he drew a circle upon the face of the deep". The medieval iconography of God the Geometer and Blake's Ancient of Days are the same gesture: the Architect delimits the world with the compass. In the lodge, compass = heaven, spirit, what has no end — as opposed to the square (earth, matter, straight limit).

Compass → Triangle

Open the compass to 60° and you build the equilateral triangle — the first perfect figure of Euclid's geometry. Francophone Masonic texts say it bluntly: the Delta "se presente tel un compasso ouvert". The two legs + the hinge = three points. The 3-4-5 of the 47th proposition is the other triangle, the one that proves the right angle. The Tetraktys is the third. All come from the same instrument.

Triangle → Base

Every triangle has a base. In the Delta of the East the base is the horizon line: the ground from which the two sides rise and the vertex touches the heights. In architecture this is the pediment. In a work this is the foundation. Without a base the triangle does not rise; without a base there is no temple. "Base" here is not a moral metaphor. It is the first constructive act: laying the line on which form will exist.

Base → Architect

The architect is who lays the base and who holds the compass. The Great Architect of the Universe is not a third god: it is the name of the function joining the two gestures — measuring the ground and tracing the heavens. Geometry was Masonry. The G in the East is that function written in one letter.

The Luminous Delta is the triangle, contains the eye, radiates light, is seated in the East with the Sun, and the figure of mere idea [garbled in the converted source].

It is not necessary to invent a letter-by-letter equivalence. The objects are already chained by the operation:

1. The Architect opens the compass.
2. The compass generates the triangle.
3. The triangle rests upon a base.
4. At the vertex (or at the center) the eye appears.
5. The eye only sees if there is light.
6. The visible light is the Sun.
7. The name of the science authorizing the gesture, and of the note that is the perfect fifth, is G / Sol.

The unified knowledge we have spoken of since the beginning is exactly this: form, star, engineer — the triangle for the mathematician, the sun for the astronomer, the note for the musician, the eye for the theologian. The lodge kept all five on the same pediment — without needing G to "mean" [passage garbled in the converted source] 1723.
