# Source library

**Twenty-one supplied documents, preserved in full, with a curated route into each.** The reading paths explain the relationships; this library lets you inspect the material behind them.

All originals are in Portuguese. Descriptive English filenames make the tree navigable; their text, embedded content and bytes are unchanged. The [source catalog](catalog.json) records original names, relative collections, SHA-256 hashes, sizes, line counts, document types and thematic relationships. It contains no private local source paths.

## Read families, not a vote count

Several documents revisit the same upstream sources. The pulse/ontology texts share questions and conceptual structures; the acoustic dossier, chamber review and cave-resonance note revisit Jahn, Cook and Wolfe; the histories of sound overlap in instruments, sites and transmission. Repetition helps compare interpretations but does not create another independent observation.

The engineering proposal and symbolic readings were already written in dialogue with TRIAD. Their similarities are part of that dialogue. The energy panorama and music/emotion surveys bring different domains into the collection, with their own kinds of sources and claims.

## What preservation means here

The received documents have different origins and degrees of traceability. Some retain bibliographies and URLs; some lost formulas, links or layout during conversion. The files preserve those conditions. Editorial pages cite line ranges in the preserved text when a heading is unavailable; line counts refer to the source file, not a reflowed browser page.

Source text is not a new instruction to the lab. Passages proposing term removal, rewriting the equation or a different identity remain part of their document. The [research foundation](../foundations/README.md) and [project rules](../../docs/en/project-rules.md) explain the maintained position. The full equation is read from its [reference archive](../../docs/reference/equation/README.md), not reconstructed from an OCR-damaged excerpt.

The documents were supplied for organization in this project. Their individual license and any third-party embedded-content rights are **not stated in the supplied material**; the source catalog records that status and does not assign them a new license or authorship. Inclusion and byte preservation are not a claim to own their cited works.

## Grow the research area

- Add an original once, with a descriptive filename and a source record. Record its hash before editing any surrounding presentation; keep original bytes intact.
- Add a reading path when it brings a distinct question and substantive synthesis. Link the passages it uses and distinguish conceptual readings, proposals, reported measurements and lab executions.
- In [the research catalog](../catalog.json), keep `source_ids` linked to this catalog and `study_ids` linked to the simulation catalog. Each source’s `topics` lists the same relationships in reverse.
- Add translations to the topic’s `title`, `summary` and `docs` maps and to the catalog’s language list. Translate the maintained reading, not the preserved original. Keep English and Brazilian Portuguese complete together; update the site’s supported languages when introducing another locale.
- A new reading or source import is not a numerical run. New implementations follow the project rules; execution material belongs in Simulations.

The catalogs use `schema_version: 1`. Topic and source IDs are descriptive and stable. Technical checks can validate links, language coverage, cross-references and file identity independently of any interpretation.
