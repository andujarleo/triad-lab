# A foundation for research

**TRIAD is nonstandard quantum physics and ontology, created by Leonardo Andujar.** Its foundational position identifies its equation with the deepest foundation of the universe and reads reality as simulation. The lab develops that inquiry through research, implementations and preserved execution records.

The equation is **unique, immutable and indivisible**. Its principles operate together:

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P1 · Oscillation** | Oscillation belongs to the constitution of the dynamics. |
| **P2 · Self-reference** | The system responds to its present state and its history: instantaneous self-interaction and memory. |
| **P3 · Coupling** | The dynamics includes relationships and exchange within the coupled system. |

The governing [project rules](../../docs/en/project-rules.md), [author’s introduction](../../docs/en/triad.md) and [equation reference](../../docs/reference/equation/README.md) provide the complete context. Reference labels such as v1.0 and v1.1 identify document revisions, not different equations.

## One universe, two places to work

**Research** connects ideas, literature, historical practices and possible applications. It makes relationships explicit: which passage suggests a connection, what kind of connection it is and which question remains open.

**[Simulations](../../simulations/README.md)** connects implementations to conditions, data, figures and reports. It records what an execution produced, including failures, contradictions and inconclusive diagnostics. A research connection does not turn into an executed study merely because a simulation link appears beside it.

Both use the shared [reference archive](../../docs/reference/README.md). A note can include ontology, a mathematical representation and a historical run; it stays intact. The reading paths explain its different roles instead of splitting its source text.

## The direction of the inquiry

TRIAD follows **self-calibration from chaos to dynamic equilibrium**. Crystallization continues within that equilibrium. New TRIAD work retains the complete equation; it neither removes terms nor tunes the dynamics externally to produce a chosen result. The project follows its own methodology and does not adopt Popperian falsification as its governing method.

Technical care serves that work: identifying the implemented operator, declaring initial conditions, checking numerical resolution, preserving files and tracing each representation to its origin. Historical implementations have their own conditions. Their maintained study pages explain reduced operators or missing inputs without changing the preserved outcome.

## How the supplied collection enters

The initial collection contains **21 supplied documents**. Some synthesize historical or scientific literature; others offer ontological interpretations, engineering proposals or reviews of earlier syntheses. Their recurring themes—process, active history, relation and dynamic organization—make a coherent research starting point.

A conceptual resemblance, a mathematical representation, a measured phenomenon and a historical transmission claim each need their own explanation. The theme pages state which relationship they are using. They keep P1, P2 and P3 together without assigning one historical object to each principle.

The originals also retain passages that recast TRIAD as a phenomenological model, suggest changing or isolating terms, or quote formulas imperfectly. Those passages document the supplied text; they do not redefine the project or amend its equation. [Source notes and hashes](../sources/README.md) make that distinction traceable.
