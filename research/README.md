# Research

**Follow the relationships. Find new questions. Return to the sources.**

Research is the reading and inquiry wing of the TRIAD universe: a place to connect oscillation, self-reference and coupling with form, history, sound, computation and human experience. TRIAD’s own foundation—nonstandard quantum physics and ontology—guides the inquiry.

[Start with the foundations](foundations/README.md) · [Meet the author](../docs/en/author.md) · [Open the simulations](../simulations/README.md)

## Three ways in

**Through experience:** begin with [Music and experience](music-and-experience/README.md), then follow [Sound and resonance](sound-and-resonance/README.md). Return, variation and response provide a familiar entrance.

**Through the idea:** begin with [Process and persistence](process-and-persistence/README.md), then [Memory and self-reference](memory-and-self-reference/README.md). Read form, history and relations together.

**Through the technical material:** begin with [Fields and computation](fields-and-computation/README.md), then [Signals and interpretation](signals-and-interpretation/README.md). Follow a representation back to its operator, state and source.

## A living collection with a readable trail

The first collection brings **21 supplied texts into seven edited reading paths**, available in English and Brazilian Portuguese. Each path connects its question to specific passages and to relevant simulation records. The original documents remain in their supplied language and exact bytes.

The [source library](sources/README.md) explains the families of texts, repeated references and known conversion problems. The [equation reference](../docs/reference/equation/README.md) and [project rules](../docs/en/project-rules.md) remain shared foundations. Research directions are identified as directions; recorded outcomes stay attached to the [simulation archive](../simulations/README.md).

New languages and themes can grow from the same sources. Keep one source identity, one preserved original and one clear route from an interpretation to its supporting material. See the [catalog and contribution notes](sources/README.md#grow-the-research-area).
