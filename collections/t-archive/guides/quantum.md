# The QM sequence

A sequence with a specification, protocols, configurations, seeds, raw data and analyses. Q00 fixes the starting point; Q01 and Q01b investigate resolution; Q02 expands to an ensemble; Q03 and Q04 examine subspaces and linearity.

| Stage | Study | Record |
|---|---|---|
| Q00 | [Frozen specification](../quantum/q00/README.md) | The canonical Theta_core specification and the starting point for the QM sequence. The hash quoted in Q00 differs from the supplied canonical file; both are preserved. |
| Q01 | [Spatial convergence](../quantum/q01/README.md) | N32/N48/N64 at fixed timestep. The recorded convergence decision is INCONCLUSIVE. |
| Q01B | [Resolution and timestep](../quantum/q01b/README.md) | N96 and timestep refinements follow Q01. The record remains INCONCLUSIVE. |
| Q02 | [A 32-seed ensemble](../quantum/q02/README.md) | An MLX ensemble with both protocol versions preserved. The recorded QM decision is INCONCLUSIVE. |
| Q03 | [Subspaces and modes](../quantum/q03/README.md) | POD/PCA and DMD examine early and late windows across four seeds. The record does not establish a shared low-rank attractor. |
| Q04 | [Linearity probes](../quantum/q04/README.md) | Two seeds compare A, B and their superposition with matched noise. The protocol deviation and INCONCLUSIVE decision are retained. |

INCONCLUSIVE decisions and protocol deviations are part of the preserved results. The earlier pilots are not relabeled as confirmatory evidence for this sequence.
