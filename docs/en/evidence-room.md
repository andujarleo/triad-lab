# The evidence room

The scientist door: every claim in this lab carries a route back to its record. Follow any number to its source.

## The chain

```text
claim → record → source → provenance
```

- A **claim** lives on a cover, edition, or study page.
- A **record** is a saved measurement, figure, or table in the study folder.
- A **source** is the code, configuration, seed, and run behind it.
- **Provenance** binds identity: hashes, migrations, and what was checked.

## Data access

- [Simulation catalog](../../simulations/catalog.json) and [research catalog](../../research/catalog.json): every study, theme, and source, with paths.
- Each study keeps a `FILES.md` index; measurements live under `results/data`.
- Saved density volumes keep their source paths and hashes; the field viewer moves through space, not time.

## Limits, stated plainly

- No simulation was rerun for the audits; the examination was static and documentary.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`, failed, and conflicting outcomes are preserved as part of the record.
- Convergence stays unresolved where the records say so; historical term-off configurations are identified, never renamed as complete runs.
