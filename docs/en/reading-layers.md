# Reading in layers

Every public page answers in three depths: one line, one minute, the full record.

- **One line** — the promise. What this is and why it matters, in a single sentence.
- **One minute** — the orientation. Enough context to decide whether to dive: key numbers, verdicts, and the route inward.
- **The full record** — the depth. Sources, conditions, measurements, and limits, with nothing restated without a link back.

## Where the layers live

- The [cover](../../README.md) carries one-line highlights; each [issue](editions.md) gives the one-minute version; study pages, audits, and sources hold the full record.
- Area indexes open with a one-minute verdict summary before the study table.
- Study pages keep their question and opening summary first (the site atlas reads them), then the execution review, then depth.

## Writing a new layer

- State facts the record supports; link the source beside the number.
- Never restate a verdict without its evidence link.
- Keep English and Portuguese together; new languages follow the [language guide](languages.md).
