A place to follow questions, published material and the next steps of TRIAD. Entries distinguish editorial changes from numerical executions so that the history remains readable as the lab grows.

## 2026-09-17 · Making the proposal visible

**Editorial and reference update. No new numerical run is reported in this entry.**

The public introduction now states the author’s central hypothesis: a deepest underlying equation, the emergence of different forms from that dynamics, and reality as a simulation. A [conceptual map](triad.md) and [vocabulary](glossary.md) connect this view to the terms used in individual studies.

The lab now carries the author-supplied [reference document labeled v1.1](../reference/equation/README.md), dated 2026-09-13, alongside the preserved v1.0 document. The labels identify revisions of the documentation. They do not identify different equations. The texts retain their wording about modes and implementations; importing them changes no recorded code, parameter or result.

The previous organization work brought **65 studies into eight areas**, with descriptive folders and links between notes, code and results. This phase adds a clearer entrance to that archive and room to expand the catalog.

### Editorial clarification · 2026-09-18

The earlier introduction incorrectly left P1 undefined and reduced P2 and P3 to memory and bath. The [glossary](glossary.md) now gives the author’s principles: **P1 oscillation; P2 instantaneous self-reference and memory; P3 coupling**. The [project rules](project-rules.md) also clarify the unique, immutable equation, dynamic crystallization and self-organization without external calibration. This is a correction of the lab’s presentation, not a change to the equation or a new numerical result.

Connecting the author’s conceptual vocabulary to declared observables remains work to document in each study. No unit or conserved quantity is invented for P1 + P2 + P3.
