# Issues of the lab

Each issue is a curated entry point: what is new, why it matters, and the route to every record. One line on the cover · one minute here · the full record behind each link.

## How an issue works

- A cover highlight is a promise: one minute of reading here, then the primary record.
- Numbers, verdicts and figures always link to their source; nothing is restated without a route back.
- Past issues stay readable; the lab never rewrites what an issue said.
- The three depths are a lab-wide convention: [reading in layers](reading-layers.md).

## Issue 01 · September 2026

**The identity issue: every study declares what it is.**

The archive holds 65 studies with very different histories: complete coupled runs, modified systems, unfinished traces, and context material. This issue answers one question for each of them, in the open: **is this record an execution of the complete TRIAD equation?** Fourteen studies document the coupled terms; fourteen depart from the reference in a documented way; twenty-five cannot be established for lack of source or run binding; twelve are context or post-processing rather than new field evolutions.

The verdicts live in two places. The [identity audit](triad-identity-audit.md) holds the study-by-study register with evidence links, in eight languages with English as principal. Each of the 65 study pages now opens its execution review with the same verdict badge and links to the audit in every language.

### In this issue

- **Visible badges on every study** — the verdict sits at the top of each execution review, next to the conditions that support it. [See one →](../../simulations/memory/memory-and-bounce/README.md)
- **Eight languages** — English (principal), Portuguese, Spanish, German, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish and Simplified Chinese. [Language coverage](languages.md)
- **A region contracts, then expands** — the r50 radius reaches its minimum at **t=3.7**. [Follow the trajectory →](../../simulations/memory/memory-and-bounce/README.md)

## Publishing the next issue

1. Choose two to four highlights that already have records; no highlight without a route to its source.
2. Write the one-minute narrative with numbers and links, in English and Portuguese together.
3. Update the cover in both languages: issue number, date, and one-line highlights.
4. Leave past issues untouched; history lives in the [lab journal](journal.md) and [provenance](../../provenance/README.md), not in rewritten issues.
5. Run the checker and the tests before announcing.
