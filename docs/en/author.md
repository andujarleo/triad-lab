# Why this lab exists

**Leonardo Andujar created TRIAD as an independent project to investigate an idea about the deepest layer of reality.** His central hypothesis is that one underlying equation can give rise to the many forms we call matter, interaction and universe.

The project’s ambition is broad: simulate different phenomena and publish the work here, making each experiment understandable to someone arriving with curiosity as well as someone arriving to inspect the code.

## The author’s point of view

TRIAD is a proposal in nonstandard quantum physics with an ontological starting point. It asks what reality is made of and explores the hypothesis that the universe is a simulation. Its vocabulary places atoms, vibration, memory and their interactions at the center of that inquiry.

The [conceptual map](triad.md) introduces that perspective. The [equation reference](../reference/equation/README.md) records its mathematical formulation. The [studies](../../experiments/README.md) show what has actually been implemented and recorded.

## A lab that grows in public

The archive contains questions tried in several forms: relationships between fields, geometry, memory, nested structures, signals and continuity. Different stages have different models and configurations. Keeping those stages readable lets others follow how the project developed.

New work can extend the archive through a specific question, a declared version of the equation and a record of the execution. Images give people a way in; code, parameters and observations give them a way to look closer.

The site and repository use English and Portuguese, with room for more languages. The intended audience includes people who want to explore the idea without running Python and people who want to examine every numerical choice.
