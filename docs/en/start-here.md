# A five-minute tour of TRIAD

**TRIAD is a proposal for nonstandard quantum physics.** Begin with a field that changes, leaves a history and responds to that history. The lab investigates how to read matter, relations and the universe through these dynamics.

## 1. Imagine three actions happening together

**Focus** concentrates. **Repulsive memory** responds to previously occupied regions. The **bath** supplies excitation and dissipation while the field evolves. The question is what the whole system does: which structures appear, how long they last and how the past changes the next response.

In the author’s analogy, focus and memory resemble opposing magnets. The analogy helps visualize their competition; equations and configurations specify its implementation. [Understand the proposal](triad.md).

## 2. Follow memory acting on the field

In [memory and bounce](../../experiments/memory/memory-and-bounce/README.md), figures track concentration, the memory response and expansion. Read the sequence as well as the final image. The record also shows where different diagnostics tell different stories.

## 3. Look for a structure inside the field

The studies of [one pocket](../../experiments/structures/a-pocket-in-the-field/README.md), [two pockets](../../experiments/structures/two-pockets/README.md) and [memory maps](../../experiments/structures/density-memory-maps/README.md) ask how a region appears, interacts and loses or retains its distinction within the volume. They bring the tour closer to the author’s central question.

## 4. Open the path that led here

The repository retains relational pilots, geometry explorations, controls and diagnostics. They are parts of the research history, with their own models and configurations. [Follow the chronology](topics/timeline.md) to understand that development.

Each study offers context and a **Files** index connecting available notes, code, configurations and results. You can read everything without installing Python.
